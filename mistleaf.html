Rooms

Three ways to stay

Every room has hot water, a mosquito net and a private bathroom. Prices are per room, per night, with breakfast.

Breakfast included

Breakfast on the balcony

Every stay includes a home-cooked breakfast: string hoppers with dhal and coconut sambol, fresh papaya and bananas from the garden, and a pot of Ceylon tea from the estate next door. Prefer eggs and toast? Just tell us the night before.

  • Served 7:00 – 10:00 am
  • Early packed breakfast for sunrise hikes
  • Vegetarian and vegan on request

Around Ella

Close to everything

We'll arrange tuk-tuks and early breakfasts for sunrise hikes. Ask us for a hand-drawn map at check-in.

Nine Arches Bridge

Short tuk-tuk ride + walk

The stone-arch railway bridge in the tea fields. Check the train times at the front desk.

Little Adam's Peak

Easy hike, about an hour up

Gentle climb with wide views of Ella Gap. Best at sunrise.

Ella Rock

Half-day hike

A longer trek along the railway line and through tea estates. Go with a guide.

Ravana Falls

Short drive down the pass

A tall roadside waterfall on the Ella–Wellawaya road.

Getting here

The train is half the trip

Send us your arrival time and we'll meet you at the station or bus stand.

By train

Ella is on the Kandy–Badulla hill-country line. Book reserved seats early in the busy season.

By road

Hire a driver from Colombo, Kandy or the south coast. Most drivers know the road to us.
