    Do I need to be home?

    No. Most customers leave a key or a door code. We note it securely and lock up when we leave.

    Do you bring your own supplies?

    Yes. We bring vacuums, mops and pet-safe, low-scent products. If you prefer your own products, just leave them out.

    When do I pay?

    We hold your card when you book and charge it only after the clean is finished.

    Can I reschedule or cancel?

    Yes, free of charge up to 24 hours before your arrival window.

    What if I am not happy?

    Tell us within 24 hours and we will come back to re-clean the areas you are not happy with, at no cost.
