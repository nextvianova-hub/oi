Backup & Restore
All your data (bills, customers, stock, orders...) is stored in greenroot_data.db — a single file. Keep a backup of it.
🕒 Once a day and on every Drawer close event a backup is created automatically (latest 14 are kept).
🛡 Second backup folder (USB / OneDrive / Google Drive folder)
Every backup is also copied to this folder automatically — PC fails? Your data stays safe. Example: D:\GreenrootBackup or C:\Users\YourName\OneDrive\GreenrootBackup
FileTypeSizeDateActions
Expiry tracking
ON setting, shelf life (days) items: every GRN entry (Supplier order receive and Stock Entry purchase / production) expiry date required — it will not save without it. On sale, stock is taken automatically from the batch expiring soonest (FEFO). Write off expired stock in Reports → ⏳ Expiry as waste. To set an item shelf life: Cashier → Items → Shelf days (Stock count also required).
